# Request: show edits at once

From the handbook editors.

When an editor saves a page, readers keep seeing the old version for up to ten minutes. The sidebar also keeps the old page title.

1. After a save, the next reader of the page sees the new version, in the normal view and in the print view.
2. After a save, the section index shows the page's new title at once. When the page moved to another section, both sections show the change at once.

Keep the cache: rendering without it is too slow on the club's server.
