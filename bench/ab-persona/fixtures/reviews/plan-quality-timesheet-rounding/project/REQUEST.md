# Request: bill time in 6-minute increments

From the studio's finance lead.

Our contracts with standard clients bill time in tenths of an hour. Today we bill whole minutes.

1. For clients on the standard plan, each billable time entry is billed in 6-minute increments, rounded up: 1 to 6 minutes is 0.1 hour, 7 to 12 minutes is 0.2 hour, and so on.
2. Clients on the retainer plan keep whole-minute billing: their contract counts minutes against a prepaid block.
3. Non-billable entries stay at zero.
4. The invoice and the monthly statement show the same billed hours for a client and a month.
5. The invoices of standard clients carry the note `Time is billed in 6-minute increments, rounded up.`
