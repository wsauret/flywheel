# Request: renew a loan online

From the head of lending.

Patrons phone the desk to renew loans. Let them renew from their account page instead.

Rules for an online renewal:

1. A patron can renew only their own loans, and only loans that are not returned.
2. A loan can be renewed at most twice.
3. A loan cannot be renewed while a hold on the item is still waiting.
4. An overdue loan cannot be renewed online. Overdue loans are renewed at the desk, where any fine is settled first.
5. A renewal moves the due date 14 days on from the current due date, not from today, so renewing early never shortens a loan.

When a renewal is refused, the page tells the patron why, in one sentence.

Out of scope: a confirmation email (the weekly loans email already lists due dates), and anything in the circulation terminal.
