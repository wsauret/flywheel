# Request: renew a residential parking permit online

From the town's parking office.

Residents queue at the town hall to renew permits. Let them renew online.

1. A resident can renew their own residential permit online from 30 days before it expires until 14 days after it expired (the grace period). Outside that window the portal says when renewal opens, or that the permit must be renewed at the town hall.
2. Renewal costs the zone's yearly fee, paid by card.
3. The new expiry date is 12 months after the old expiry date, also when the resident renews during the grace period, so the grace days are not free.
4. The permit records the date of its last renewal; the clerks' monthly report reads it.

Out of scope: business permits. They need a document check, and they stay at the town hall.
