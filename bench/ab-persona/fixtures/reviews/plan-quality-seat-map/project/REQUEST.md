# Request: tell held seats from sold seats on the seat map

From the box-office manager.

The seat map shows a seat as free or taken. A customer cannot tell a sold seat from a seat that another customer holds for a few minutes while paying, so they give up on performances that are not sold out.

The seat map shows every seat in one of four states:

- free: anyone can pick it;
- held: another customer holds it; the map says it may come free in a few minutes;
- sold;
- house: the theatre keeps it back. It is never offered for sale online.

Nothing else about booking changes: a held, sold, or house seat still cannot be picked.
