# Request: choose where a refund goes

From the shop managers.

Today every refund goes back to the card the customer paid with. Customers often ask for something else, and the cashier has to refund the card and then sell a gift card by hand.

When a cashier refunds a sale at the till, they choose one of three destinations:

1. The original payment method. This stays the default, so a till that sends no choice keeps today's behavior.
2. A gift card: either a new gift card printed at the till, or an existing gift card that the customer hands over.
3. Store credit on the customer's account. This is offered only when the sale was made to a customer account.

The refund record shows which destination the money went to, so the end-of-day report can separate card refunds from the rest.

The rules for how much can be refunded do not change.
