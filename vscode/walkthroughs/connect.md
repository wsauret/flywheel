## Connect a provider

Flywheel runs the model you already pay for. It does not resell one, and it has
no account of its own.

Pick a provider in the Flywheel panel and it will ask for the one thing that
provider needs: an API key, a browser sign-in, or a short device code. The
credential is stored by Flywheel on this machine and is never sent anywhere
except to that provider.

You can connect more than one and switch between them later with
**Flywheel: Model and effort**.

Flywheel itself lives at [github.com/wsauret/flywheel](https://github.com/wsauret/flywheel).
