# anti-slop LLM-judged rules (vendored source)

- Source: https://github.com/K-Mistele/anti-slop, a fork of anti-slop by Dillon Mulroy (MIT, see `LICENSE`).
- Commit: `b7c1619b4d51b4815e89ba3700a975a6ec54b8c3`.
- Contents: the Effect question preset (`src/jev/effect-rules.ts`, `src/jev/plugin.ts`), the Martin Fowler question preset (`src/martinfowler/jev-rules.ts`, `src/martinfowler/jev-plugin.ts`), the changed-file runner (`src/jev/lint-changed.ts`), and the diff rules (`src/diff-rules/*.ts` without their tests), copied verbatim and then changed as the ledger below states. They run on the vendored plugin in `scripts/oxlint-plugin-jev/`. `tests/integration/scripts/anti-slop-diff-rules.test.ts` covers the diff rules' decision backend switch. `owned-commands.ts` and `pre-push.ts` are Flywheel's own files, not vendored: the owned-process runner of the judge command and the hook, and the body of the opt-in pre-push hook `.githooks/pre-push`, which `tests/integration/scripts/anti-slop-pre-push.test.ts` covers.
